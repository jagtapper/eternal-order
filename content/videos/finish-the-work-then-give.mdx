*Karma yoga* gets sold as a mood of sacrifice. The older instruction is dryer.

Do the work fully. Keep what you produce. Then, if you give, give because you can, not because a crowd took it first. *Daan* completes the philosophy. Forced redistribution is a different machine.

Agency first. Ownership of outcomes. The freedom to keep or share. That is how a house stays able to help the next house.

If the guilt creeps in when you finish something, read the four aims again. Prosperity under dharma is not a personality flaw.
