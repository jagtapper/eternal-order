Self-reliance is not a mood of toughness. It is a structure of capacity. On this site it sits under *artha*.

The short walks a simple arc. Dharma first. Then artha as legitimate means. Then pancha mahabhuta, the five elements, gathered in the house as panchamrita: milk, curd, ghee, honey, sugar.

Earth is substance. Water is flow. Fire is refinement. Air is gathering. Space is the field.

The map is a model you can live. Not a church you join.
