In the Eternal Order, *prana* is the force that keeps a life lit. Attention is the tap. You can point it. You can also leave it open and call that freedom.

Scattered attention is not a moral failure first. It is a leak. The body reads it as low-grade threat and stays noisy. Deliberate attention does the opposite: the store can actually settle.

This is why *manas* without *buddhi* never lands, and why *citta* fills with weather. The inner instrument is how memory is built. Attention is how the instrument is aimed.

The longer map is [Citta, manas, buddhi, ahamkara](/education/citta-manas-buddhi-ahamkara).
