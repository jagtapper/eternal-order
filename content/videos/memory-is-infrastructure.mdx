A road is infrastructure. So is a port. So is a story a child can still recite when the adults have grown tired of themselves.

This short is a claim. Memory is not a museum. It is the part of a people that holds the weight.

## Watch for

The thread across the page. That is the image: one filament of continuity through a dark sheet.

If you take only one sentence, take this. A people stays together by remembering how.
