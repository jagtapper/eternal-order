A useful pair in the old language: inner stillness, inner motion. *Shiva* as the one who does not flinch. *Shakti* as the heat that actually moves. You need both. Stillness without heat is freeze. Heat without stillness is weather.

*Buddhi* is the cut: this, not that. That cut is easier when there is heat in the system. When the heat is gone, holding the group story is cheaper than holding yourself.

This is not a call to burn the house. It is a call to notice whether you still have the warmth to discriminate, or only the overlay.

The inner map is [Citta, manas, buddhi, ahamkara](/education/citta-manas-buddhi-ahamkara).
