*Satya Yuga* is called the age of truth. Not a holiday. A condition: speech, act, and inner life still match. Attention is conserved. Prana is not spent on noise.

The later yugas are a thinning of that. Kali is not only a date. It is leak as weather. What used to be default has to be practiced.

People wait for a golden age to arrive on a calendar. That is a Kali habit. The work is pointing attention at the moves that matter, now. The four ages describe how memory thins. They do not excuse waiting.
