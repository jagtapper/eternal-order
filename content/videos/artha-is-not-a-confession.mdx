Most people meet the Eternal Order as temples and ritual. Fine as far as it goes. It is not the whole map.

One of the four aims is *artha*: material means, earned. Not a spiritual bypass. Not a sin to be washed off. You own the fruit of the work. No one else is entitled to it as a first principle.

That is older than any modern school that treats wealth as original dirt. The old thought treated *unbound* appetite as disorder. It did not treat skill as theft.

If you have been waiting for permission to build, the map already gave it. The restraint is dharma. The means are still yours to make.

The longer lesson is [Dharma, artha, kama, moksha](/education/dharma-artha-kama-moksha).
