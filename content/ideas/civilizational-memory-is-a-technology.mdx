A people can lose a city and remain themselves. They cannot lose the stories that teach them how to stand and remain themselves for long.

That is a practical claim, not a sermon.

## What gets stored

The four aims, the epics, a household rite, even the names of a jati: these are storage. They pack a hard ethic into something a large people can carry without a ministry.

Anyone who has shipped software already knows the feeling. A protocol nobody can remember will not be kept.

## What goes wrong

Memory fails in two ways. Forgetting, which is obvious. And pulling the wrong file, which is how slogans replace duties.

The filenames we try to keep honest here are simple. Dharma, artha, kama, moksha. Varna and jati. Exile and conflict. Self-rule. The five elements.

## How it moves

A free journal is just a way to pass a file without a membership form. If an idea needs a donation button to survive, it was not yet an idea.

Read. Send it to someone. Argue with the map. That is the whole method.
