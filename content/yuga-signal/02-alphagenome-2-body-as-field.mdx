Google DeepMind announced AlphaGenome 2. It predicts how DNA variants affect gene regulation in a specific tissue, cell type or cell state, and it was trained on more than 100 million single-cell profiles across 68 tissues.

On the TenK10K immune dataset it identified 30% of causal variants against 16%, and 43% of effect directions against 23%, compared with the original AlphaGenome fine-tuned on single-cell data. These figures come from DeepMind's announcement. The model is available for non-commercial research through the AlphaGenome API. DeepMind says it is for research use only and is not validated for clinical or diagnostic use.

## Why it sits here

Every cell carries the same DNA, yet each cell type reads it into a different form. AlphaGenome 2 learns that shared code so it can predict how one change shows up in each form. The verse's image is the same: one substance, many forms. The Upanishad's own point is knowing the one cause of all things; this is a comparison of images, not a claim that it describes genomics.

## The Yuga signal

**Health span.** Tracing nearly twice as many causal variants to the cells they act in is an early step on a long road toward health span. It is a research tool only, not validated for clinical or diagnostic use, and it treats nothing yet.
