On Nov 20, 2026, Levent Alpöge released a Lean formalization of a claimed proof of the Riemann hypothesis, developed with Claude. Andrew Sutherland's MIT page reports that the formalization compiles and is accepted by Lean's kernel and two independent kernels. Alpöge says he intends to write it properly.

A short film on the claim is here: [Bhau's reel](https://www.instagram.com/0xbhau/reel/DgBrqezTCmc/).

## The parallel

> "Who is the greatest knower of Truth among you?" … He decided to put questions and see what answers would come from Yājñavalkya.

Brihadaranyaka Upanishad 3.1.1–2 (Swami Krishnananda). King Janaka offers a prize to the best knower of Truth. Yajnavalkya claims it at once, and the assembly then tests him question by question.

## Why it sits here

A bold claim to the hardest knowledge, made first and then tested in public, is where this claim stands now. In SDIO's reading this sits beside the yuga accounts as a parallel consistent with cyclical time: knowledge is claimed, then tested in open inquiry. It is not a claim that the text describes mathematics.
