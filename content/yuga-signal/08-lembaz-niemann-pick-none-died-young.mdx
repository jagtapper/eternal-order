On Nov 17, 2026, the FDA approved Lembaz (adrabetadex) for patients whose Niemann-Pick type C neurological symptoms began before age 6. It is the first drug shown to help these patients live longer. It is given by injection into the spinal fluid every two weeks, for patients weighing at least 7 kg.

The FDA looked at 36 treated patients from three studies and an expanded access program, compared with a matched external control group. Treated patients had a 55% lower risk of death, and median survival was 8.5 years against 4.9.

**The disease is still fatal.** The comparison was against an external control, not a randomized trial. The drug carries a boxed warning for hearing impairment, including permanent hearing loss.
