Archer Aviation says its Midnight aircraft received an FAA Market Survey Special Airworthiness Certificate. It allows piloted operations from Hawthorne Airport in Los Angeles in the coming weeks. This is an experimental certificate, not a type certificate.

**The certificate covers conventional runway takeoffs and landings only.** Midnight has not yet flown a piloted transition from hover to wingborne flight, which Archer targets for the end of 2026, and a separate approval would be needed for vertical takeoff and landing. Archer also reports more than 300 miles of piloted city-to-city flights around the San Francisco Bay Area in October, all with runway takeoffs and landings. Passenger flights are targeted for 2027.

## The parallel

In Yuddha Kanda 6.121.7–10, Rama says the road back to Ayodhya is hard to travel on foot. Vibhishana answers that he will take him to that city in a single day, in the Pushpaka: Kubera's aerial car, "shining like the sun", that goes wherever one wishes. The fuller description follows at 6.121.23–30, where it moves with the speed of the mind.

## Why it sits here

The verses are about turning a long overland journey into a single day. That is the promise of an air taxi, and Midnight is a long way from keeping it. Setting human flight in a new form beside an Itihasa account of flight in another age of the cycle adds context. It is a literary parallel, not a claim that Valmiki described an aircraft or that Midnight is a vimana.

## The Yuga signal

**Mobility.** Piloted city-to-city test flights and an FAA step toward Los Angeles operations point at time won back from travel. The certificate is experimental and covers runways only. There has been no piloted hover-to-wing transition, and passenger service is targeted for 2027.
