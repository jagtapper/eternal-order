On Starship Flight 15, which lifted off from Starbase, Texas on Nov 13, SpaceX says the Starship upper stage reached orbit and deployed 26 Starlink V3 satellites. After more than 20 hours in space, it burned out of orbit and flew back to the same launch site it had left 22.5 hours earlier. On Nov 14, the tower's arms closed and caught Starship for the first time.

**The booster did not come back.** Its boostback burn ended early on low fuel mass, with the root cause under investigation. It aborted to an offshore landing, and its flight termination system was triggered. SpaceX has not said whether the caught ship will fly again.

## The parallel

In Uttara Kanda 41, the Pushpaka returns to Rama and announces itself as the vimana come back from Kubera's palace (41.3). Rama honours it, then sends it off: go now, and come back when I remember you (41.14½, paraphrased from the Gita Press Hindi). The live air-taxi card used the Pushpaka for the journey (Yuddha Kanda 6.121). This card is about the return: a craft that goes, comes home, and stands ready to fly again.

## Why it sits here

A vehicle meant to come back and be used again is what reuse means, and SpaceX has now caught an upper stage returning from orbit. In SDIO's reading, this is a parallel consistent with cyclical time. It is a comparison, not a claim that the text describes a rocket. Proportion matters: this was one first catch on a test flight, not routine service.

## The Yuga signal

**Cost of access to orbit.** An orbital upper stage came home and was caught for the first time, which points toward cheaper launches through reuse. It is still a test flight: the booster was lost this time, no reflight of the caught ship has been announced, the full stack has not yet been recovered, and SpaceX gave no cost figure.
