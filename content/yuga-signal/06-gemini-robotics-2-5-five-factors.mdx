Google DeepMind introduced Gemini Robotics 2.5, which it describes as its most capable vision-language-action (VLA) model to date. A VLA model turns camera input and spoken or written instructions into robot motor control.

DeepMind says it evaluated the model on 200 distinct tasks, such as serving food with tongs, tying a bow on a gift box and pipetting a liquid. Against its own previous baselines it reports a 30% to 100% higher success rate, depending on the task, and 20% to 30% faster completion. In a proof-of-concept with Apptronik and Mercedes-Benz, an Apollo 2 humanoid sorted automotive parts into sequencing trays on production lines in Kecskemét, Hungary and Sindelfingen, Germany, with a 96% success rate.

**All figures are company-reported, and no technical paper accompanies the post.** Ars Technica reports that the model runs on cloud servers, and DeepMind's Kanishka Rao told Ars that closing the remaining gap to human reliability will be a "steep road." The model is rolling out to robotics partners and is not generally available.

## The parallel

> And there was no manual labour. And then the necessaries of life were obtained only by being thought of… And during that yuga, there was neither disease, nor decay of the senses.

Mahabharata, Vana Parva 3.148.12–14, where Hanuman describes the Krita age to Bhima (Section CXLVIII in Ganguli's translation; in the Sanskrit, *kriyā nāsīc ca mānavī*, "there was no human toil").

## Why it sits here

The model's headline use is taking manual work off human hands: in the pilot, a humanoid sorted car parts on a production line. The Vana Parva names the absence of manual labour as a mark of the Krita age. In Eternal Order's reading the two sit side by side as a parallel consistent with cyclical time. One factory pilot is a long way from that age, and this is not a claim that the Mahabharata predicted robots.
