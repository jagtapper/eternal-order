The *Ramayana* is not a children's pageant. It is a drill: what does a person do when the easy path and the right path split?

Ayodhya offered Rama the throne. Kaikeyi, by a promise Dasharatha could not break, offered him the forest. He went. Not because exile is romantic. Because his *svadharma*, in that hour, was to keep his father's word.

Bharata then refused the stolen crown. He would not live another man's dharma. He ruled as steward of Rama's paduka until the rightful king returned. A man who cannot refuse a throne that is not his will not be able to hold a kingdom that is.

## The fork has a name

The *Katha Upanishad* states it without ornament:

> *shreyash ca preyash ca manushyam etah... shreyo hi dhiro 'bhi preyaso vrinite*
> (Katha 1.2.2)

The good and the pleasant both approach a person. The steady one discriminates and takes the good. The dull one takes the pleasant, to keep what he has.

Rama chose *shreyas*. Ravana chose *preyas*: another man's wife, another man's honor, appetite dressed as kingship. The epic is not good people versus bad people. It is two uses of power.

Even Ravana's counselor saw it. Maricha warned him: *ramo vigrahavan dharmah* — Rama is dharma given a body (Valmiki, Aranya-kanda 3.37.13). To fight him is to fight the order that holds a people up.

## Then he gathered who would stand

Rama's war did not begin at Lanka. It began when he did not waver in the forest. Then he built an alliance the palace could not have imagined: Sugriva, Hanuman, the vanaras, Vibhishana. Not sameness. Coordination under a shared purpose. Each in his *svadharma*.

That is the same logic as Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj and the 96k. Many roles, one Swarajya. The American founding said it in another tongue. Self-rule is not a mood. It is a people who will not outsource their own order.

Michigan's *preyas* is comfortable fragmentation: my language group, my mandir board, my calendar, my WhatsApp circle. It feels like survival. It is Ravana's logic in miniature.

Michigan's *shreyas* is harder: teach the epics as living law, stand in *svadharma*, ally across sampradaya without erasing lineage. This fortnight some temples marked Vinayaka on one panchang while Maharashtrian mandals wait for Ganesha on September 14. Same Isa. Different clock. Do not turn the calendar into a purity test.

Rama did not wait for a unanimous Ayodhya. He kept dharma, then gathered who would stand. Unity in community is that sequence. Not a meeting. A choice.
