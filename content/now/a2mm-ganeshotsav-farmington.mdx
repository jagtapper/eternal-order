On **Saturday, Sep 19, 2026**, the Ann Arbor Marathi Mandal (A2MM) held its Ganeshotsav at **Farmington High School**, 32000 Shiawassee St, Farmington. The mandal’s own events calendar lists the day as Ganapati 2026, 11:00 AM to 5:30 PM, at that address.

A Marathi-language recap published Sep 25 by *Iye Marathichiye Nagari* describes the day in order. ShivShardul Percussions opened with dhol-tasha for the arrival of Ganapati. Children installed Ganesh murtis they had made with their own hands. A traditional Marathi meal followed, with ukadiche modak, varan-bhat, poli, and batata bhaji. The afternoon program, *Swar-Rang*, featured vocalist **Ashwini Gore-Deshpande**, who travelled from Austin, Texas, for A2MM, with **Giri Aigalikar** on tabla, **Praneet Marathe** on harmonium, and **Uma Kanade** as compere. The day closed with aarti and visarjan.

## Why it belongs here

Michigan Now records Sanatan Dharma and Hindu-community developments in this state. A named Michigan mandal, a named public venue, and an independent newsroom write-up are that lane. The recap’s own framing is cultural continuity: language, music, food, and a festival calendar handed to the next generation, with children making the murtis themselves. This desk reads it as Eternal Order philosophies kept alive in practice far from Maharashtra, not as a sermon and not as a headcount.
