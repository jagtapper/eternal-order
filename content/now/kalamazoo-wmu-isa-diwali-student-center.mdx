On **Saturday, Nov. 21, 2026**, the **Indian Student Association at Western Michigan University** hosts **Diwali 2026: Festival of Lights** from **5:00 to 9:00pm** in **Student Center Ballrooms A and B**, **1070 Student Center Loop Rd, Kalamazoo**.

The university's listing describes Diwali as the victory of light over darkness, knowledge over ignorance, and good over evil, with Indian food, games, cultural performances and a DJ. Free food is listed. The invitation is addressed to the "WMU family," so this is an evening for the WMU community rather than a public event.

## Why it belongs here

Michigan Now records Sanatan Dharma and Hindu-community developments in this state. A named student organization putting the festival of lights, and its principle of knowledge over ignorance, into a Michigan public-university room is that lane. It is the West Michigan counterpart to the Michigan Tech Diwali at the Rozsa Center.
