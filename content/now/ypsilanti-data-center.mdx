On Tuesday, Sept 1, Ypsilanti City Council gave preliminary approval to an emergency 60-day pause on new zoning permits for data centers. The ordinance still needs another reading before it is final.

The city had posted a [public-hearing notice](https://cityofypsilanti.com/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=2747) for that meeting at One South Huron Street. The pause would let staff study energy, water, and health effects. The city’s zoning code does not now define data centers.

## City, not Township

This is the City of Ypsilanti. It is not Ypsilanti Township, and it does not bind a University of Michigan project across that line. Council member Patrick McLean told [WEMU](https://www.wemu.org/wemu-news/2026-09-02/emergency-data-center-moratorium-one-step-closer-to-passage-by-ypsilanti-city-council) the ordinance has little immediate practical effect.

## Why it is on this page

A named Michigan governing room, a concrete land-use step, and a second reading still ahead. This desk does not call the pause final.
