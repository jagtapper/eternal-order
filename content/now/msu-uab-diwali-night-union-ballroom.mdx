On **Sunday, Nov. 8, 2026**, the **Michigan State University Activities Board (UAB)** held **Diwali Night** in the **MSU Union Ballroom**, its first in two years. The evening had food, music and tables lit with candles.

UAB brought the event back after **We Are Saath MSU** submitted an event request, The State News reported. UAB outreach and inclusion coordinator Katy Winkler said the night gave students who celebrate a time to come together, and gave others a chance to learn something new.

The report noted that Diwali is marked across several traditions: Hindu families recall Rama's return to Ayodhya after fourteen years of exile, with Rama's victory standing for dharma; Jains mark Mahavira Nirvana Divas; Sikhs mark Bandi Chhor Divas. Saath president Deepinder Kaur, who is Sikh, summed up the shared thread: "light always wins over darkness."

Michigan Now records Sanatan Dharma and Hindu-community developments in this state. A Michigan public university's own programming board restoring a festival of lights at a student group's request, in its central union, is that lane.
