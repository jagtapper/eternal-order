On Sept 1, Wayne State and the Barbara Ann Karmanos Cancer Institute said eligible adults with metastatic pancreatic adenocarcinoma can now receive daraxonrasib (Rasonque) at the Detroit center.

The FDA approved the oral RAS-targeting tablet on Aug 26 for adults who have already had at least one systemic therapy, or who are not candidates for multiagent chemotherapy. Karmanos was one of the sites on the international RASolute 302 trial. Najeeb Al Hallak, M.D., M.S., of Wayne State and Karmanos, led the Detroit arm.

## What the room said

The trial, in 500 previously treated patients, reported median overall survival of 13.2 months on daraxonrasib versus 6.7 months on standard chemotherapy. Severe side effects were common in both arms. This desk does not call that a cure, and it does not widen eligibility past the FDA label.
