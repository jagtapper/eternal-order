On Sep 7, Bharatiya Temple Chairman Dinesh Billore wrote members and devotees inviting them to a fundraising dinner on **Saturday, Sep 26, 2026**, in support of temple development and maintenance on Adams Road in Troy.

This is a Michigan Hindu-community institution putting its facilities work in public. Fundraising Committee Chair **Dr. Surekha Rao** is named as leading the evening. The letter lists concrete projects already moving: illuminated entrance signage completed; a well and irrigation system in progress for Golden Jubilee landscaping; kitchen renovation nearing completion; parking-lot expansion, a yoga center, and renovation of the old school building into a heritage center in design.

## Why it belongs here

Michigan Now exists for Sanatan Dharma and Hindu-community developments in this state. A named Troy temple, a named chairman, and a dated facilities pipeline are that lane. The dinner is the public ask attached to the work. This desk records the room and the projects, not a sermon and not a donation pitch.

The source is the Chairman’s email from `bttemple.it@troy-temple.org` to members, Sep 7, 2026. Tickets and support links live on the temple’s own site, [b-temple.org](https://b-temple.org/). Distinct from the Aug 28 Troy civic presser already on this rail.
