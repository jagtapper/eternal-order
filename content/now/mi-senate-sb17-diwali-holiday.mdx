On **Thursday, Nov. 5, 2026**, the **Michigan Senate** passed **Senate Bill 17**, sponsored by **Sen. Michael Webber**, which would designate **Diwali, the festival of lights,** as a state holiday. The roll call (#268) was **33 yeas, 0 nays, 5 excused**.

The bill was introduced Jan. 22, 2025, and referred to the Committee on Regulatory Affairs. On Nov. 5 the Senate approved discharging it from committee and passed it the same day. The House received it that day and referred it to its **Committee on Government Operations**, where the House companion, Rep. Sharon MacDonell's **House Bill 5838**, has sat since April 21, 2026. The Senate Fiscal Agency completed its analysis of the Senate-passed version on Nov. 6.

SB 17 is not law. It still needs action in the House and the governor's signature.

Michigan Now records Sanatan Dharma and Hindu-community developments in this state. The first floor passage of a Michigan Diwali-holiday bill is an official state-room action on the Eternal Order's festival of lights. This desk records the vote and the referral, not a forecast.
