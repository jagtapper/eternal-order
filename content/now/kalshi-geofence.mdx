On Sept 2, Attorney General Dana Nessel said an Ingham County judge had entered a preliminary injunction requiring KalshiEx, LLC to keep geofencing in place so Michigan residents, and people physically in Michigan, cannot reach its sports contracts. The [AG release](https://www.michigan.gov/ag/news/press-releases/2026/09/02/ag-nessel-secures-second-order-halting-unlawful-kalshi-sports-wagers-in-michigan) says violations would carry a $500,000-a-day fine.

The underlying lawsuit, filed in March, alleges Kalshi offers unlicensed sports betting under the Lawful Sports Betting Act. A June temporary restraining order had already paused the operation. The case is not a final judgment on the merits.

## What this desk will not say

Kalshi did not “lose the case.” Not every contract class is decided. This is an access order while litigation continues.

## Why it is on this page

A Michigan court, a state regulator, and an immediate statewide change in who can open those contracts.
