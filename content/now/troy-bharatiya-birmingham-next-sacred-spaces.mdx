On **Thursday, Nov. 19, 2026, from 10 a.m. to 1:30 p.m.**, **Birmingham Next** runs a local **Sacred Spaces** trip by bus to **Bharatiya Temple in Troy**. The group leaves from Birmingham Next, 2121 Midvale St., Birmingham.

Per the official listing, visitors are escorted by a guide and cover the basics of the temple's philosophies and the practice of murti, with an up-close visit to the murtis. If time permits, a priest will perform a short ceremony for the visitors. The trip involves steps, and footwear comes off before the sacred area, so the listing asks visitors to bring socks. There is a fee for members or guests; the listing does not show the amount.

## Why it belongs here

Michigan Now records Sanatan Dharma and Hindu-community developments in this state. A Birmingham community program taking a public group into a named Troy temple, on an official listing, is a civic room meeting the Eternal Order's philosophies. This is a separate event from the temple's Sep 26 fundraising dinner already on this rail.
