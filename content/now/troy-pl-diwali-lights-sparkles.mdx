On **Thursday, Nov. 5, 2026**, **Troy Public Library** hosts **Diwali Lights & Sparkles** from **6:00 to 7:30pm** in the **Meeting Room** at **510 W Big Beaver Rd, Troy, MI 48084**.

The library calendar lists a teen-friendly craft evening: decorate a CD into a sparkling diya centerpiece with a tea light; pom-pom garlands; 3D lanterns; and mandala coloring. Vegetable samosas and chutney are offered while supplies last. The listing frames the hour as light, color, and art inspired by Diwali traditions.
