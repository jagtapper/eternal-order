On **Monday, Nov. 9, 2026**, **University of Michigan Electrical and Computer Engineering** holds a **Diwali celebration** from **4:00 to 5:00pm** in the **EECS Atrium** at the **EECS Building, 1301 Beal Avenue, Ann Arbor, MI 48109**.

The ECE events page says the gathering includes music, crafts, and a henna artist, plus food. Guests are asked to bring an MCard. The same page states the event is **free and open to the public**.

## Why it belongs here

Michigan Now records Sanatan Dharma and Hindu-community developments in this state. A named Michigan public-university room putting Diwali — a festival of lights — on a campus calendar and opening the door to the public is that lane. This desk records the room and the hour, not a sermon and not a headcount.
