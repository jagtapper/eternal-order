On **Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026**, the **Indian Students' Association at Michigan Technological University** holds **Diwali 2026** from **4:30 to 7:30pm** at the **Rozsa Center for the Performing Arts, 1400 Townsend Drive, Houghton**.

The university's event page describes an evening of cultural performances, music and festive energy to celebrate the festival of lights. The event is ticketed: general tickets are $20 for MTU and $22 for non-MTU through Nov. 12, then on-spot based on availability. Contact: isacomm@mtu.edu.

## Why it belongs here

Michigan Now records Sanatan Dharma and Hindu-community developments in this state. A named Michigan public-university performing-arts room hosting Diwali, listed on the university's own platform and open to non-MTU ticket holders, is that lane — and this desk's first Upper Peninsula card.
