On **Thursday, Nov. 5, 2026**, the **City of Novi** holds its annual **Diwali Fest** at **Novi Civic Center**, **45175 Ten Mile Rd, Novi, MI 48375**, from **5:30 to 8:30pm**.

The city's own page frames the evening as cultural performances, food, local vendors, and community celebration. The 2026 flyer lists lamp lighting by the mayor at 5:45pm, an opening dance titled "Leaders of Light" at 6:30pm, and fireworks at 8:15pm. Free shuttles run from the Novi High School stadium lot on Taft Rd beginning 5:15pm; the last shuttle leaves the Civic Center at 8:45pm. No strollers.

## Why it belongs here

Michigan Now records Sanatan Dharma and Hindu-community developments in this state. A named Michigan city putting Diwali — the Festival of Lights — on a civic calendar at a public civic-center room is that lane. This desk reads the night as light, culture, and community on a Michigan public square, not as a sermon and not as a headcount.
