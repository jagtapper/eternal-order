On **Saturday, Nov. 14, 2026**, the **Uttar Pradesh Association of Detroit (UPAD)** holds its **Diwali 2026** celebration, **"Deep Jale, Dil Mile,"** at **5:00pm** at **North Hills Middle School, 3456 Lahser Rd, Bloomfield Township**.

UPAD's event page describes an evening of performances, food and festivities. Tickets start at $25 for adults. UPAD is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit; its president is Sunila Kumar and its chairperson is Anand Sinha.

The only source for this event is **UPAD's own event page**. The school building is the venue; this is not a school-district program, and no district listing was found.

## Why it belongs here

Michigan Now records Sanatan Dharma and Hindu-community developments in this state. A named Michigan community association bringing Diwali, the festival of lights, into a public school room is that lane. This desk records the room and the hour, not a headcount.
