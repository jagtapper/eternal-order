The **Genesee County Board of Commissioners** passed **RES-2026-1290** on **Sep 23, 2026**, recognizing **October 2026 as Hindu Heritage Month**. The item was co-curated by the county's DEI Commission.

The resolution names Flint institutions directly. It cites the "Sri Paschima Kasi Viswanatha Temple, (also known as Kasi Temple Flint), which holds the historic distinction of being the first Shiva Temple established in the mainland United States, founded in 1979." It also names the India Club of Genesee County and Chinmaya Mission Flint, whose Tapovanam ashram was inaugurated in 1990.

## Why it belongs here

A county board putting Flint's Dharmic institutions into the public record is the civic room this desk follows. The recognition runs through Oct 31.
