On **Saturday, Sep 26, 2026**, the Maharashtra Mandal of Detroit (MMD) held its Ganesh Chaturthi program at **Detroit Country Day School – Upper School**, 22305 W 13 Mile Rd, Beverly Hills.

The mandal’s events page now links an official photo album for the day. The frames that are public show rangoli on the grounds, dhol-tasha percussion, and an outdoor procession at the school — Marathi festival practice carried in a Michigan school courtyard.

## Why it belongs here

Michigan Now records Sanatan Dharma and Hindu-community developments in this state. A named Michigan mandal, a named public school venue, and an org-posted album are that lane. This desk reads the day as cultural continuity — language, rhythm, and a festival calendar kept alive far from Maharashtra — not as a sermon and not as a headcount.
