*Rta* is older than the courtroom. In the early hymns it names the order that makes seasons, vows, and speech trustworthy.

## Source, then paraphrase

The Vedic use is not a statute book. It is closer to a claim about the world: there is a grain in things, and human conduct can run with it or against it.

Paraphrase: *rta* is the order that precedes enforcement.

## Interpretation, marked as such

Modern writers often flatten *rta* into "natural law" or "the rules." That is tidy and a little wrong. Law is what you write when the grain is no longer felt. *Rta* is the felt grain.

This digest does not prove a policy. It restores a distinction. If we keep using dharma as a sticker, we should at least remember the older word that dharma was asked to protect.
