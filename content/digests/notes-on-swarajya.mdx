*Swarajya* is easy to print and hard to keep honest.

## What it can carry

Self-rule of a people who already know themselves. The refusal to wait for a distant court to assign you a self. The bind that made the Maratha lesson more than a raid.

## What it should not carry

It should not be a brand for a personality. It should not be a demand that every other community disappear. It should not replace competence.

This digest is a fence. The word is useful inside the fence. Outside it, the word becomes noise.
